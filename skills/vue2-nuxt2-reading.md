Read the legacy Vue 2 / Nuxt 2 app as a specification of behaviour, never as a structure to copy. Before porting a slice, record its cases in docs/characterization/<slice>.json with a legacy file:line source for each (static when read from code, observed when captured). Look where behaviour hides: asyncData and fetch (client or server), route middleware and guards (redirect order), mixins (shared lifecycle and implicit state), filters, watchers and computed values (side effects), Vuex modules (parallel modules, mutation order), the event bus, $refs and direct window or document access (focus, scroll), directives, and client plugins that inject $ helpers. Replace mixins with hooks, the bus with callbacks or typed actions, filters with pure functions, DOM mutation with refs. Record a visible change you notice in docs/migration/findings.md and keep the old behaviour. Keep docs/migration/map.md true.
